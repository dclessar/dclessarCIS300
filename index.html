Who am I?

Hey! I'm Derek Lessard. If you're here, reading this text from my website as you are right now, that probably means you've met me in some capacity. Or maybe you've known me for a while, or even since the day I was born! I'm currently 20 (birthday on Jan 30th, 2006) and I'm in my 3rd year of ASU. I'm studying Media Arts and Sciences (Music) and currently minoring in Percussion Performance. I love to play and perform as well! I'm in the Sun Devil Marching Band, marching in independent indoor groups such as Vision Percussion and Flux Indoor Percussion. I like to edit photos and videos, and I'll be showcasing them here in the media page! I also keep a journal of things on my mind in the journal page if you'd like to take a gander through there! Just for fun, I've made a Q&A page if you'd like to fill out a form answering some of my questions (uno reverse card from typical Q&As!). And (if I have the time) I have the history page if you'd like to learn more about my heritage and family! I hope you enjoy my digital garden!
